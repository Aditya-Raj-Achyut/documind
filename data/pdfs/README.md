Put your PDF files in this folder (15-30 PDFs from ONE domain works best).

Ideas for a domain that makes your project stand out:
- Indian government scheme guidelines (PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat, Skill India...)
- Annual reports of 5-10 listed companies
- Your own university's rules / syllabus / placement policy
- RBI / SEBI circulars and master directions
